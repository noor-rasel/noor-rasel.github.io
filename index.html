Noor M. Rasel

PhD Researcher in Medical Physics
Department of Physics, University of Texas at Arlington

My research focuses on nuclear medicine imaging, including theranostics. I work closely on advanced image reconstruction techniques, Monte Carlo simulations, and artificial intelligence-driven algorithm development for SPECT and PET systems, focusing on attenuation correction (AC), motion correction, denoising in gated acquisitions, and image guidance of dosimetry for emergent targeted alpha therapy (TAT) techniques.
